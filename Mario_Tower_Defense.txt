🍄🎯 Game Concept: Super Mario Tower Defense
Bowser is sending waves of minions through the Mushroom Kingdom to capture Peach
again. But this time, Mario and friends are prepared—with defense towers!
Players will strategically place towers based on Mario universe characters and mechanics
(e.g. fire flowers, Koopa shells, warp pipes) to stop the enemy waves.

🧱 Levels of Complexity

🟢 Level 1: Basic (Mushroom Plains)
Theme: Classic green hills and Goombas
●​ Path: Linear dirt road​
●​ Towers:​
○​ Fire Flower Tower: Shoots fireballs at enemies​
○​ Koopa Tower: Throws shells that bounce and hit multiple targets​
○​ P-Switch: Temporarily slows enemies on the map​
●​ Enemies:​
○​ Goombas, Koopa Troopas, Paragoombas​
●​ Gameplay:​
○​ Drag-and-drop towers on grid​
○​ Simple health bar and wave counter​
○​ Basic currency (coins) for kills​
●​ Victory Condition: Survive 10 waves​

🟡 Level 2: Intermediate (Desert Dunes)
Theme: Dry Dry Desert–like world

●​ Path: Winding with forks (multiple paths, enemies choose randomly)​
●​ New Towers:​
○​ Yoshi Egg Launcher: Slows enemies by eating one every 10 seconds​
○​ Boo Tower: Invisible until enemies approach, deals heavy damage​
●​ Enemies:​
○​ Pokeys, Bullet Bills, Buzzy Beetles​
●​ Gameplay:​
○​ Tower upgrades (e.g. Fire Flower → Fire Burst Tower)​
○​ Special abilities (e.g. Star Power boost)​
○​ Heat-based traps (Sunburn Tile slows enemies)​
●​ Victory Condition: Survive 20 waves, some with mini-bosses​

🔴 Level 3: Advanced (Bowser’s Lava Castle)
Theme: Magma, traps, and chaos
●​ Path: Branching + teleport tiles (Warp Pipes)​
●​ New Mechanics:​
○​ Pipe-based routing (place Warp Pipes as strategic reroutes)​
○​ Boss Fights (Boom Boom, Bowser Jr., Bowser at wave 30)​
○​ Tower fusion: Combine towers for super attacks (e.g. Fire Flower + Koopa =
Fire Shell Launcher)​
●​ Enemies:​
○​ Magikoopas (teleport), Hammer Bros (ranged), Lakitus (summon enemies)​
●​ Gameplay:​
○​ Tower skill trees​

○​ Tower targeting modes (first, strongest, closest)​
○​ Resource management (coins + power-ups like mushrooms)​
●​ Victory Condition: Defend Peach’s castle for 30+ waves​

🌍 Worlds (Themed Zones)
1.​ World 1: Mushroom Kingdom (Grassland)​
○​ Friendly look, simple maps​
○​ Ideal for intro/tutorials​
2.​ World 2: Desert Dunes (Dry Dry Ruins)​
○​ Sandy tiles, quick enemies, more paths​
○​ Environmental hazards (sandstorms, quicksand)​
3.​ World 3: Bowser’s Castle (Lava Land)​
○​ Red-hot tiles, fewer build spots​
○​ High difficulty, frequent bosses​
○​ Warp pipes, lava hazards, explosive traps
